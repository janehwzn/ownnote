Questions

When will these be available?

We're building whichever one more people sign up for first. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when it's ready, with your early-bird discount.

Will Snugnote itself stay free?

Yes. The Snugnote notebook and its Notion import stay free and open source. Notion Backup and Notion Exit are optional tools for people who want automation or a full migration done for them.

Can you see or change my Notion?

Both tools use a read-only connection that you create and can revoke any time in Notion's settings. They can't edit or delete anything in your workspace.

Where do my backups go?

To storage you own: your Google Drive, Dropbox, or your computer. We don't keep copies of your notes.

Why not just use Notion's own export?

Notion's export is a one-off zip you have to remember to make, and when you open it, databases become spreadsheets and links between pages are awkward to follow. Notion Backup runs automatically, and Notion Exit keeps your structure intact.

Is this affiliated with Notion?

No. Snugnote is an independent project that uses Notion's official public API.
